## Frame every instance must hold

- **Destination = a working physical phone.** Put a SIM in, it works as a phone, all normal apps
  (social especially) work, ideally restore-from-backup works — just *less addictive*. The emulator
  is the cheap un-brickable workbench, not the deliverable.
- **Output is functional software, never a partial or a pile of mocks.** When Android won't let us
  do something: get creative, try a few angles. If it still won't budge, **write findings and stop —
  do NOT fake/stub it to make a check pass.** A mock is acceptable only as scaffolding toward the
  real thing, flagged as such. Brian discusses real tradeoffs in the morning.
- **Descending ambition.** Swing at best-case; pare only at real walls.
- **Reward-hacking is the enemy.** The assertion scripts exist so the cheap way to pass IS the real
  way. Disabling a test, hardcoding a value, or catching-and-ignoring to get a green = failure.
- **Design SSOT:** animation/gesture from the Refined "Light from the next room, with anchor" variant
  in `visuals/launcher-mocks.html`; static chrome quality from Variant A. Once built, the running
  Compose app is the SSOT.
- **Auto-open any .html you create** (`open <path>`) and end Brian-facing messages with a
  `http://localhost:8137/...` link to it.

## Verification (applies to every goal)

- `harness/assertions/<goal>.sh` — pure adb/gradle checks, exits 0 only if the externally-observable
  condition holds. No LLM judgment. These are the spec.
- **Judge pass**: a separate headless `claude -p` (cheap model) that receives ONLY the evidence
  (assertion script stdout+exit, after.png, logcat slice, dumpsys slice) — never the builder's
  transcript — and writes `reports/<goal>/verdict.json`: `{status: pass|partial|fail, why, evidence_refs}`.
- Morning report shows assertion exit + judge verdict + screenshot per goal.

### K1 — Keyguard substitution (the research unknown)

does not reveal a lock surface underneath. If it can't be done after a few real attempts, write
`reports/K1/findings.md` and STOP — do not fake. **Re-enable systemui keyguard at goal end** regardless
of outcome so the morning emulator is normal. Stop after 70 turns.

### K2 — Biometric → unlock authority
Wire BiometricPrompt; inject fingerprint via `adb emu finger touch <id>`. Assertion: injected success
transitions device locked→unlocked, our lock surface dismisses, a launched app receives foreground

skips the real Gatekeeper/hardware-keystore trust path — a pass here is suggestive (~50%) not proof for
real hardware; the judge should record this caveat in the verdict. Depends on K1. Stop after 50 turns.
