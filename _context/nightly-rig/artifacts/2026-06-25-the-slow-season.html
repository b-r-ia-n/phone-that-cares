Recover. Follow what's alive.

Deferred maintenance on the nervous system. Meditation, exercise, therapy, the friends, the water. This is not the runway before the work — for these weeks it is the work. A week or two of structurelessness is good; you said so. Take the cheap version of it now, on purpose.

No applications. The front door isn't open yet, and you don't want what's behind it in June.

Augside doors

Let the side doors appear.

A job is a bundle of problems someone wants solved badly enough to pay for it. Your best fits have no listing — they're the Brian-shaped holes. You don't find those by applying; you find them by being visible and specific. One public artifact if it wants to be made (the far-UVC essay, only if it's alive). One generous, particular email when one is genuinely earned. Quality of one over volume of fifty.

Groundwork, not a sprint. If a week passes with none of it, the season is still on schedule.

Now you may rush, a little.

Listings, interviews, the calibration of “is $160k in the Bay real.” It keeps. Your ideal was an offer you don't start until October or November — which means the work that earns it can be unhurried and still land on time. A company that wants you but isn't in a hurry is a small red flag; so is a search that's in a hurry with nothing yet to be hurried about.

The card I drew tonight was slow down, and your own situation had already drawn it first. There is nothing this summer that rewards speed. The thing you're trying to find — right livelihood that plays well with meditation and a life — is, almost by definition, not the thing you'd grab if you were rushing.

So: let it be a season, paced like one. The sun above is low on purpose. Hang this where the panic-to-apply can see it.
