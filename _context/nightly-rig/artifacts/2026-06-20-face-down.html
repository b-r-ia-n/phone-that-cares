Tap the phone to set it face down
The back glows a slow, warm ember — a hearth, not a screen. It says this person chose to be here to everyone at the table, and gives them nothing to pick up. The light is for the room, not the owner. It can't be swiped, checked, or scrolled.

Display-layer. Ungameable. The gesture you already make, finally answered.
