The Body While Scrolling — Parts Asking for Things

Brain (Dopamine System)
Variable-ratio reward schedule. Each scroll is a pull of the slot machine.
"Again. One more. Again."
Adrenal Glands
Cortisol spikes from outrage content and social comparison. Chronic low-grade stress.
"Am I safe? Am I enough?"
Eyes
Blue light suppresses melatonin. Reduced blink rate dries corneas. Accommodative strain.
"Please look away."
Heart / ANS
Sympathetic activation from stimulating content. Heart rate variability decreases.
"I can't tell if this is danger."
Neck & Spine
Forward head posture adds 27–60 lbs of effective load on the cervical spine.
"I wasn't built for this angle."
Thumb & Wrist
Repetitive scroll motion. De Quervain's tenosynovitis rising in under-25s.
"This tendon has limits."
Circadian Clock
Screen use past 10pm delays sleep onset by 30–60 min. The body loses its sense of night.
"What time is it, really?"

Every part of the body is asking the user to stop. The phone never does.
