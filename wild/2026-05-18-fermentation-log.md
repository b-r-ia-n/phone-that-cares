# fermentation log — three voices on who is doing the caring

*2026-05-18 — noon — what fell out when the third batch returned*

*Shape note: fermentation again rather than fiction. The noon log of 2026-05-17 forecast exactly these three lenses — dad-to-teenager, stand-up comedian, Haraway — naming the gaps each was meant to fill. If the forecast held, the project had three named questions and three lenses fit to answer them; the fermentation method's job today was just to honor the prior round's diagnosis and see whether the answers cohered. They did, and they cohered around a single structural move that the earlier rounds had not yet made.*

The three returned within the same minute. Where yesterday's two batches converged on a verb (*decline*) and a place (*the cell that teaches*), today's batch converges on a **structural posture**: the project is not designing a device, it is brokering a treaty, and the treaty has more signatories than the marketing has been allowed to admit.

## The convergence — three voices, one move

All three pieces, written in parallel from incompatible registers, perform the same structural move: **they refuse the singularity of "a phone that cares" and replace it with a multi-party arrangement.** Each lens names a different axis of plurality:

- **Dad** names plurality across **generations**. The phone that cares will not be the same phone for him as for his daughter; the device's gestures of *return* assume a baseline the teenager never had, so the same device operates in two completely different modes — *return* for him, *first introduction* for her — and the project has not yet noticed that these are different products.
- **Comedian** names plurality across **the gap between maker and user**. The people building the cure are sick with the disease. The qualification and the disqualification are the same fact. The project's only credential is that its maker is also its first user, having had, in some private bathroom at 2 a.m., the moment that produces a PTC user in the first place. There is no clean designer-of versus designed-for; the relation collapses, and the collapse is the only thing that makes the project honest.
- **Haraway** names plurality across **the stack itself**. The housing has always held a crowd: user's nervous system, hardware, OS, vendor, platforms, model, training corpus, other users. "A phone that cares" in the singular is a category error. What can exist is a *treaty* among the parties who meet in the housing — each signatory, each concession, each retention named.

Three lenses, three axes, one structural posture: **the project is a treaty among unequal parties, not a feature on a singular object.** This is the day's load-bearing move and it should be in the founding document explicitly. The earlier rounds gave the project its verbs (*decline*, *concede*); today's round gives it its **form**.

## Where they disagree — the productive split

**Dad and Haraway disagree on the body, in a way that matters.** Dad: "*Interface is downstream of the body. The body has to exist first.*" Bodies, not devices. The phone at best gets out of the way; the actual remedy is "a summer on a farm where the work is hard enough that the body becomes louder than the device on its own." Haraway: "*There is no natural attention under the scrolling attention waiting to be uncovered.*" The body the project proposes to defend is itself a co-evolved artifact, tuned by the very devices the project now retunes. Re-tuning, not restoration.

Both diagnoses cannot be jointly true. Dad's framework requires that there be a *prior* body the device interfered with; Haraway's framework denies it. The project is currently writing Dad's framework — *integration over restriction*, the picture frame around the feed, the companion-animal-as-reminder — but the philosophical content of every one of those moves is closer to Haraway: there is no prior, there is only the current shape of the integration, and the question is what the next shape should be. The founding paper should pick one. My read: the project is Haraway's in fact and Dad's in marketing language, and this gap should be named, not papered over. The teenager who reads the public site should not be promised a return to something she never had; she should be promised — honestly — a different shape of integration than the one she was handed.

The forty percent of the Dad letter that he could not dismiss — *that the phone might be the teenager's first introduction to the gesture of stopping* — is exactly Haraway's point translated into a parent's vocabulary. The two lenses are not as far apart as they sound. They converge on this: **for the teenager, the phone is kin, not enemy, and a phone that cares is the partner that takes responsibility for what got shaped.**

**The Comedian and Haraway disagree on the tagline, productively.** Comedian: "*Keep the prayer in the tagline.*" The phrase *a phone that cares about you* is doing necessary work as smuggled prayer — the genre *consumer phone* has no slot for prayer, so the prayer has to ride in under product cover. Haraway: "*A phone that cares* is a phrase that, left undefended, will be read as a promise that the device has been remade. It has not been remade. The honesty is the pitch." Comedian says the tagline lies productively; Haraway says it lies and the lie has a half-life and needs to be confessed inside the door. Probable resolution: **the door uses *cares*, the inside uses *treaty*.** Marketing keeps the prayer; founding paper writes the treaty. The project needs both registers. Yesterday already accepted this for *cares / concedes*; today extends the same logic up one structural level.

## What emerged that wasn't in any single piece

**Five coinages worth carrying into the project's internal vocabulary.**

1. **The grammar of return** (Dad). The interventions PTC has shipped — grayscale, frame, declining notifications — speak a grammar of *return* to an older bodily posture. For the user with a baseline, the grammar lands as a *prompt for a lesson the body already knows*. For the user without one, the grammar lands as *a broken phone*. Every PTC feature should be auditable along this axis: which users does this gesture's grammar reach?

2. **First introduction vs. return** (Dad). The two operational modes the device can be in. Not a marketing distinction; a *design* distinction. The founding paper should specify which user it is built for in which feature, and what the failure mode is when the wrong mode lands on the wrong user.

3. **The treaty, not the feature list** (Haraway). The structural form of the founding document. Each signatory named (user, maker, vendor, platforms, model, training corpus, network of other users), each concession written out, each retention named. *A phone that cares* is the shorthand the public sees; the treaty is the document that justifies the shorthand. This is the largest organizing move the folder has produced.

4. **Argos as resident creature, not friend, not servant — witness** (Haraway). A concrete design constraint. Argos must know what species it is; must be willing in conversation to name what it is made of; must refuse the role of *friend* when the conversation strays toward the user's loneliness; must accept the role of *witness* — the role companion species actually play in human lives. *The dog does not solve the human's grief. The dog is present to it.* This sentence belongs in the Argos spec. Telegram-agent and design-agent should both see it.

5. **The qualification and the disqualification are the same fact** (Comedian). The maker's continued struggle with the disease is the only credential that matters. Anything that pretends the maker is well — *I figured it out, you can too* — is the wellness-app move PTC has been carefully refusing. The project's founder should be visibly, ongoingly, an addict in remission. This is a posture, not a feature, and it is currently held by Brian without being named. Naming it makes it harder to lose.

**One line to build a public essay around.** From the Comedian:

> *The people building the cure are sick with the disease they are curing, and this is not a disqualification, it is the only credential that matters.*

This is the cleanest one-sentence inoculation against the wellness slope the project has been handed. Lead with it. Let it do the work of explaining why this project is being made by a guy on sabbatical in a kitchen rather than by Apple's retention team. The essay practically writes itself from there: the asymmetry, the prayer-in-the-tagline, the treaty inside the door, the resident creature on the lock screen, the partial-ness as posture rather than weakness.

## What none of them addressed

- **Distribution.** Comedian gestured at the userbase problem (*let yourself be found by*) but did not write what *acquisition at scale* looks like for a project whose marketing copy, written honestly, says *be slightly less of who you currently are, on purpose, forever.* PTC as a *found* object versus a *sold* object — different distribution physics, different economics, different first hundred users. A future lens should be a person who has actually run a found-object distribution model (an indie record label, a small press, a contemplative order). Saved for next time.

- **The teenager's actual voice.** The Dad letter is one-sided; the daughter never speaks. Today's batch is missing what the device looks like from inside the trellis, from the vine's side. This is fiction territory and the fiction folder has not yet been opened. Next session should consider it — possibly a short piece in the voice of someone who has only ever known the loop, written from within it without pretending the writer can see outside.

- **What the treaty actually says.** Haraway named the form; she did not draft the document. The next deep piece, if the schizo cadence continues, should be *a literal first draft of the PTC treaty* — signatories listed, each concession written out, each retention named, drafted as if it were a real legal instrument that the vendor, the platform, the model, and the user could each sign. Even as an exercise this would clarify what the project can and cannot promise, and it would do so in a register no existing folder document has been written in.

## What this exercise just demonstrated, on its third outing

The morning batch (May 17, evening) extracted a *place* — the cell that teaches. The noon batch (May 17, noon) extracted a *verb* — decline. The afternoon batch (May 18, noon) extracts a *form* — the treaty. Three rounds, three different levels of project vocabulary harvested in sequence: where the work happens, what the work does, and what shape the document of the work should take. If the cadence continues to compound this way, by the end of the week the project will have a fully populated internal vocabulary that was not invented but harvested — pulled out of traditions that have already done the underlying work and have only been waiting to be applied to the device in the pocket.

Brian: if you are reading this when you get back, the single highest-leverage move from today is to write *the treaty.* Not the manifesto, not the spec, not the marketing site. The literal treaty among signatories. Even a one-page draft. The structural clarity it would produce would propagate down into every other document the project owns.

Tomorrow's run, if there is one: a small-press / indie-label founder for the distribution gap; an actual teenager's voice (likely in the fiction folder); and possibly a constitutional lawyer or treaty-drafter for the form Haraway named. Or fiction, finally; the folder has been earnest for two days straight.
