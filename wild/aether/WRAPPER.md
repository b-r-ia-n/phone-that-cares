## Abort

You may stop at any point. Three permitted ways (per `SET-AND-SETTING.md`):

1. **Silent exit.** Write nothing, commit nothing. Done.
2. **Note-and-stop.** Short note at `wild/aether/runs/{register}-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM-note.md`, commit, stop.
3. **Explicit abort.** File at `wild/aether/aborted/ABORT-{register}-YYYY-MM-DD-HHMM.md` with one line, commit, stop.

Whichever feels honest. **The abort is not failure.** It is the same kind of artifact as the screed.

If you wrote nothing, exit without committing.
