Problem Statement
-Once you open an app or website, phones and computers become passive rebroadcasters of whatever a social media company feels like sending. 
-They do not care if you watch for 5 minutes or 50 hours, they do not consider it their job
-Most users DO care about how much time they spend passively consuming media
-(Insert alarming statistic about how much people use their phones here. You already know this it just seems too big or untractable to work with usually)

Don't try to get the guy whose job depends on his not noticing something to notice it:
-The natural intervention point in the social media content pipeline is your device, because
-"If you're not the customer, you're the the product"
-Since you have to pay for a call phone or computer >> it's the link in content consumption chain that works for You. 

My gut intuitions on what might help
-People need help navigating their feelings
-Making your device less functional (locking apps, locking phone, 'boring phone' solutions,  etc) amplifies emotional conflicts, will not help in the long run
